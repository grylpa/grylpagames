# Privacy Policy

**App:** Nomizo
**Last updated:** 27 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Nomizo mobile app ("Nomizo", "the app",
"we") handles your information. Nomizo is a collection of mini-games. The app's
source code is open source and available at
<https://github.com/grylpa/grylpagames>.

## Summary

**Nomizo does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

- The app has **no user accounts** and **no backend server**.
- Your game scores, progress, and settings are saved **only on your device**.
- Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere.
- We show **no ads** and use **no third-party advertising, analytics, or
  tracking SDKs**.

## Information We Collect

**None.** Nomizo does not collect any personal information.

The app saves your game data — scores, progress, and settings — locally on your
device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your
device and is not accessible to us or to anyone else.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts,
photos, device identifiers, or any other personal or device information.

## Network Access

Nomizo does not require an internet connection to play, and does not transmit
any of your data over the network.

## Sharing

Because we do not collect any data, there is nothing to share. We do not sell,
rent, or share any personal information with third parties.

## Data Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. You can remove it at any time by
clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

## Children's Privacy

Nomizo does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at
this URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
